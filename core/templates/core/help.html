📖 How to Use BillSplit

1

Create a Group

Go to the homepage. Enter your group name (e.g. "Goa Trip") and your name. Select currency. Click Create Group.

💡 Tip: Use a descriptive name so everyone knows which group it is.
2

Share the Link

After creating the group, click the "🔗 Copy Link" button. Send this link to all your friends on WhatsApp.

💡 Tip: Make sure EVERYONE joins before anyone adds expenses. This ensures fair splits.
3

Friends Join the Group

When friends open the link, they see "Enter your name to join". They type their name and click Join Group. No account needed!

⚠️ Important: Ask everyone to join FIRST before adding any expenses. If someone adds an expense before others join, the split won't include them.
4

Add Expenses

Click "+ Add Expense". Fill in:

  • Who Paid — select the person who actually paid
  • Amount — how much was paid
  • Title — what was it for
  • Split Among — select ALL members (very important!)
  • Split Type — Equal (default) or Custom amounts
  • Payment Proof — upload screenshot or mark as cash
⚠️ Important: Always select ALL members in "Split Among" unless the expense was only for specific people.
5

Record Personal IOUs

If a friend bought something personal (clothes, food) but asked you to pay — that's a personal IOU, not a group expense.

Click "+ Record Personal IOU" in the group page. Select who lent and who borrowed. Add amount and reason.

💡 Tip: IOUs are automatically included in the final settlement calculation.
6

Check Balances & Settle Up

The Balances section shows each person's net position:

  • Green = gets back money
  • Red = owes money
  • Grey = all settled

The "Settle Up" section shows the minimum payments needed to clear all debts.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

Adding expense before everyone joins
Ask all friends to join first, then start adding expenses.
Not selecting all members in split
Always check all members in "Split Among" for shared expenses.
Using personal expense as group expense
Use Personal IOU for personal loans, not Add Expense.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Share the group link at the start of the trip
  • Add expenses immediately after paying
  • Always attach payment proof for big amounts
  • Use custom split if shares are unequal
  • Record IOUs immediately so you don't forget
