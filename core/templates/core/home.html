Our algorithm calculates each person's net balance (total paid minus total owed). Then it pairs up the biggest creditor with the biggest debtor and settles them one by one. Result: minimum number of payments needed. For example, 5 people with 15 expenses typically settle with just 4 payments instead of 15!
Sometimes a friend buys something personal (clothes, footwear) but doesn't have cash. You pay for them. This is a personal loan — not a group expense. Record it as an IOU and it automatically gets included in the final settlement calculation.
